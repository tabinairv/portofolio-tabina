Award-Winning Project: Blue Carbon Website Ranked Top 3

International Student Competition on Blue Carbon Counting in Mangorve Ecosystem, Indonesia

December, 4 2023

I participated in the International Student Competition on Blue Carbon Counting in Mangrove Ecosystem, Indonesia, where my team and I developed a website called Carpenter. The website functions as a user-friendly tool to estimate blue carbon storage in mangrove ecosystems based on field data and scientific parameters. Through this project, I combined my knowledge of coastal resource management with web development skills to promote awareness of carbon sequestration in mangroves. This experience allowed me to explore the intersection of technology and environmental science, and contributed to our achievement as one of the top three winners in the competition.
